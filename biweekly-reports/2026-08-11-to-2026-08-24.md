# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 11–24 August 2026

**Status:** In progress — evidence recorded through 17 August 2026

## Executive summary

The work progressed from initial exploration to a more structured problem analysis.
The CPS narrative was reorganized, a solution-neutral description of UTxO capacity
was developed, and Cardano mainnet data was analyzed to provide an empirical basis
for the discussion. In parallel, the collected solution proposals were organized into
a common design framework. No final architecture or policy recommendation was selected.
The next steps are to review the CPS, validate the empirical methodology, complete the
comparison of alternatives, and gather the remaining application evidence.

## Goals

### 1. [Define the min-UTxO problem and formal model](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/9)

**Actions completed**

The CPS was reorganized to present the current mechanism, the role of persistent UTxO
state, and the application-facing problem more clearly. The analysis separated the
need to keep ledger state bounded from the specific way the current min-UTxO rule is
represented. It also described outputs as occupying persistent capacity and
transactions as the operations that allocate and release that capacity.

**Next two weeks**

Obtain domain review of the revised narrative,
verify the remaining assumptions, and ensure that the CPS stays focused on the problem
rather than prescribing a solution.

### 2. [Produce and validate the mainnet evidence](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/10)

**Actions completed**

A local full-history Cardano DB-Sync environment was prepared and used to reconstruct
UTxO creation, consumption, and live-set counts by epoch. A deterministic historical
sample was also used to estimate the minimum-ADA requirement under the rules that
applied when outputs were created. At completed epoch 648, the analysis recorded
11,076,329 live UTxOs and estimated a creation-era minimum-ADA requirement of
14.348 million ADA, with a 95% interval of 14.168–14.529 million ADA.

**Next two weeks**

Document the methodology in a reproducible
form, review the historical sizing assumptions with Ledger experts, and compare the
deterministic sample with independently selected samples.

### 3. [Construct complete candidate architectures](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/16)

**Actions completed**

The collected proposals were reorganized around four questions: how capacity is
controlled, how economic settlement works, how the obligation is represented in the
ledger, and how a transition from existing state would work. This provides a common
structure for describing alternatives without treating any single proposal as already
selected.

**Next two weeks**

Complete the candidate descriptions, apply a
shared evaluation framework, and document their security, accounting, liquidity,
compatibility, and governance trade-offs.
