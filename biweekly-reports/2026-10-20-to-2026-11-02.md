# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 20 October–2 November 2026

**Status:** Interim draft as of 27 October 2026. The reporting period is still in progress.

**Tracking:** [Backlog](../backlog/README.md) · [M1 — CPS](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/3) · [M4 — Ledger prototype](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/4).

## Executive summary

The DepositStore prototype completed its current executable business-rule suite:
206 checks passed on 23 October, followed by 209 after the historical-output-store
integration on 26 October. The model now retains each live output's accepted size
and creation-time pricing, with an exact total for its recorded deposits. Later
refactors passed targeted checks; the complete regression run for the latest
working tree remains pending. Discussion with Will and Pi clarified the benefit
of removing the deposit/size feedback loop and exposed decisions still needed on
Plutus visibility, numeric declarations and simpler pricing. Next steps are to
stabilise the implementation, validate representative application flows and resolve
those choices before consolidating the CIP design. CPS-0037 remains under review.

### 2. [Consolidate the output-split impact and accounting requirements](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

On 20 October, the prototype completed its current transaction-rule scope:
declarations, exact accounting, release authority, settlement destinations and
minimum-coin behaviour. Store-backed outputs can contain native assets, a datum,
a reference script or only an address with zero application ADA; their deposit is
funded separately. Legacy outputs retain their existing minimum-coin requirement.
[Settlement and authority](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/ade9da28f33f3fe74dbeb5c281728dfc96ab2b33),
[minimum coin](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/a384c4333f8b276968702face92bb5b608f1425b)
and [collateral contracts](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/75403c2a23b674416ef9a29549d4a6c795dc7242)
were committed. Release authority follows the spending rules; this does not create
a separate recovery right for the original deposit funder.

The [23 October checkpoint](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/88a68589d7385a159551241171efa33851f1f782)
completed the active ledger-effect contracts for UTxO selection, allocation records,
fees, ADA conservation, translation and epochs. It also fixed output decoding to
preserve the received serialized size, including TopTx, SubTx and collateral-return
outputs. Re-encoding can produce a different size and therefore the wrong deposit.

On 26 October, the
[historical-output-store integration](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/04ae12ff5bc0a711e2857a43f5721bba1b6559e6)
made one map authoritative for live outputs and their deposit metadata. The stored
accepted size, formula version and creation-time rate determine the historical
deposit released on spending. The cached total must equal the deposits associated
with live store-backed outputs, replacing the earlier allowance for an unexplained
surplus. Snapshots preserve that metadata; legacy entries have no separate deposit.

Recorded validation is tied to its implementation checkpoint:

| Checkpoint | Recorded result |
| --- | --- |
| 20 October, transaction rules | All 178 transaction checks passed; eight Store-specific collateral contracts also passed in their focused run. |
| 23 October, complete active business suite | 206 checks passed with no pending cases; three accepted-size regressions and five transaction round-trip properties passed. |
| 26 October, historical store | 209 business checks, 14 metadata/size properties and 127 golden checks passed, with additional bounded state and compatibility checks. |
| 27 October, generic stored-output refactor | 165 targeted checks and a 100-case bounded UTxOState round-trip property passed. The later accessor rename compiled. |

The latest committed checkpoint is
[`f3533831b`](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/f3533831bbab8c23b2ac7bd9f59bfb5db6f21dbe).
Further module and deposit-calculation changes remain local and uncommitted, with
their combined validation pending. The 209-check result does not cover all these
later edits. The active suite was reorganised and narrowed during development;
passing it does not mean all originally proposed pricing, staking, voting or
inherited collateral cases are implemented.

**Next two weeks**

Finish the current refactor and rerun the complete business suite and relevant

claiming a complete migration policy. Review the strict refund-settlement rule
identified by Will, and distinguish coverage of that rule from agreement on the

work, assess storage and lookup costs, and keep the deferred pricing, staking,

### 3. [Review transaction netting and downstream integration](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41)

**Actions completed**

Will shared the
[incremental integration workspace](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-minutxo-integration/commits/minutxo-incremental/)
on 20 October. Discussion then concentrated on what scripts and builders should
see and which protocol changes bring a measurable benefit:

- **Pricing and builder complexity.** Pi
  [confirmed the benefit of removing the fixed point](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C0CATC4PQ59/p1792653683972129)
  and suggested conservative fixed allowances for assets while retaining datum and
  reference-script sizes. This is a candidate simplification; its constants and
  safety tradeoffs are not an adopted pricing rule. Will also relayed Polina's
  suggestion to compare a formula-only change with the larger architectural change.
- **Declarations.** Pi questioned repeating a net amount the ledger can derive.
  Nicolas [agreed to investigate](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C0CATC4PQ59/p1792656865480539)
  separating the numeric amount from the explicit choice of delegation and release
  destination. The implemented prototype still carries exact declarations.
- **Plutus and payment semantics.** Will's
  [ADA-payment examples](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/D06FTPHMSNN/p1792761312903689)
  challenge whether a split simplifies some flows while complicating others.
  On 27 October, Nicolas reported feedback from Nicolas Biri favouring access to
  total value, application assets and deposit; Will was still considering the
  implementation. The script view and compatibility contract remain open.
- **Collateral sponsorship.** Pi supplied a
  [Babel/LP workflow](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C0CATC4PQ59/p1792654309751309)
  where a SubTx provider would fund TopTx collateral while the user retains
  transaction assembly. This was explicitly identified as follow-on work outside
  the current deposit scope.

The seven-minute stream update deck and speaker notes were prepared and refined,
then [shared with Will on 22 October](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/D06FTPHMSNN/p1792661836760729).
The [presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1MkhHosZppIGvu2XqFVcxd5yGmh9jZQEH_BOfLAqP2os/edit)
explains the before/after output model, netting and SubTx delegation. Preparation
and sharing are confirmed; delivery of the talk is not established by this review.

**Next two weeks**

Use concrete ADA-only payments, token transfers, script transitions and sponsored
SubTxs to compare the existing representation, an in-output split and separate
Store accounting. Agree the Plutus view and the role of on-chain numeric
declarations with Will and Polina, incorporating Pi's and Philip's feedback.
Pin the downstream workspace to the stabilised prototype and record accepted
