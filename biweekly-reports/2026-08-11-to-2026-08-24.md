# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 11–24 August 2026

## Executive summary

After the initial orientation and scoping phase, work moved into the current
`cardano-ledger` codebase to trace the consequences of the existing representation and
understand the likely change surface of the requested work. In parallel, the stream
continued reconstructing the unwritten Buildfest proposals and documenting where
participants' views differed. The CPS reached a published problem-analysis checkpoint
with mainnet evidence through completed epoch 648 and a framework for comparing
implementations. The official backlog, milestones, and reporting structure were also
published. Both the design and code tracks remained exploratory: no architecture or
policy recommendation was selected. The next priorities were domain review,
reproducible evidence, complete candidate comparisons, and further code-level
feasibility work.

## Goals

### 1. [Define the min-UTxO problem and formal model](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/9)

**Actions completed**

The CPS was reorganized to establish the persistent-resource lifecycle before the
application-facing abstraction problem. It defined output count and serialized bytes
as separate operational dimensions, described transactions as the allocation and
release boundary, and expressed the current economic projection as
`U(t) = 160N(t) + S(t)`. It distinguished supply-derived financing ceilings from
measured node limits and made the missing deadline-based benchmarks explicit. A
separate defensive model treated cardinality growth and serialized-state growth as
different scenarios, separating recoverable capital, fees, throughput, cleanup, and
hardware thresholds. The revised CPS and its evidence visuals were published to
`main` on 19 August in commit `3a457bb`.

**Next two weeks**

Obtain domain review of the formal model, derive valid current-era output-size bounds,
and replace hardware orders of magnitude with declared node-service benchmarks.
Review the public scope so the CPS describes the application-facing problem without
absorbing the alternatives report or prescribing a settlement mechanism.

### 2. [Produce and validate the mainnet evidence](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/10)

**Actions completed**

A local full-history Cardano DB-Sync environment was used to reconstruct creations,
consumptions, net change, and the exact live set by epoch. A deterministic historical
sample estimated the requirement under the rules applicable when each output was
created. At completed epoch 648, the analysis recorded 11,076,329 live UTxOs and
36.556 billion ADA in the live set. The estimated creation-era minimum-ADA requirement
was 14.348 million ADA, with a 95% interval of 14.168–14.529 million ADA; the analysis
kept exact counts, sampled monetary estimates, and staking proxies distinct. The
defensive model reused this frozen baseline and documented that current hardware
guidance does not establish `N_DoS` or `S_DoS`.

**Next two weeks**

Publish the DB-Sync version, frozen tip, SQL, scripts, and assumptions in a
reviewable form. Validate historical sizing against Ledger-native logic, reproduce
material estimates with independent samples, and keep descriptive mainnet evidence
separate from causal claims about the mechanism.

### 3. [Construct complete candidate architectures](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/16)

**Actions completed**

Because the Buildfest discussions had produced no shared written design record,
follow-up conversations with participants were used to reconstruct the proposed
mechanisms, their assumptions, and the points on which views differed. The resulting
material was reorganized around four independent questions: capacity control,
economic settlement, Ledger representation, and transition. Required invariants were
separated from implementation choices. The analysis covered the current embedded
minimum ADA, Ledger-held deposits, credential backing, output-distributed backing,
state charges and credits, explicit caps, capacity tokens, and lazy migration. It
established that fees alone do not bound the stock of live state and that a guaranteed
state-reduction credit requires solvent backing.

**Next two weeks**

Complete internally coherent candidate descriptions, then apply one evaluation
framework covering security, solvency, accounting, liquidity, staking,
compatibility, governance, and migration. Keep exploratory prototypes as evidence
about feasibility rather than treating them as selected policy.

### 4. [Align the domain model with the adopted specification](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/26)

**Actions completed**

Following the high-level investigation, the current `cardano-ledger` codebase was
examined to determine what the requested separation would mean in practice: which
types encoded the existing coupling, which interfaces would be affected, and where
compatibility constraints would appear. Exploratory prototype work restarted from a
clean upstream base on 21 August. A small separated-output model first tested `Assets`
and `CapacityDeposit` on real Conway outputs. By 24 August, `cardano-ledger` commit
`23e2ae8` generalized the historical `MaryValue` coupling behind
`MaryValueRepresentation`, creating a reviewable boundary for the Dijkstra experiment.
This was a feasibility checkpoint, not completion of M4 and not evidence that a
solution had been adopted.

**Next two weeks**

Integrate the output-role split into Dijkstra while preserving legacy wire forms,
total ADA, era translation, and collateral correctness. Add focused representation,
translation, CDDL, and accounting tests, and feed any design constraints back into
the alternatives and solution-specification work.
