# 04 — Write the final policy recommendation

**Milestone:** [M2 — Implementation Design Space Analysis & Policy Recommendation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/2) · **Status:** Proposed

## Outcome

A reviewed implementation design-space analysis and final policy recommendation
selecting the architecture that the CIP must specify.

## Acceptance criteria

- Report explains methodology, evidence, candidates, comparison, and limitations.
- One complete architecture is recommended with a clear decision rationale.
- Rejected alternatives and minority views are documented.
- Open risks, governance implications, and validation needs are explicit.
- Team approval establishes the input contract for M3.
