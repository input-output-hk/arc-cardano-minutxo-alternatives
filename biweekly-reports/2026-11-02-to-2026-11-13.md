# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 2–13 November 2026

**Status:** Final for the period ending 13 November 2026.

## Executive summary

The Ledger prototype can now balance deposits for a transaction and its nested
transactions. Its full PR workflow passed on 12 November. The team split the
design into two draft CIPs so that changing how builders calculate minimum ADA
can be evaluated independently of moving that ADA into a separate deposit.
Will tested the first pricing formula against 229.5 million historical outputs.
The next decisions concern the extra ADA the formula requires and what existing
Plutus validators should see when they spend a deposit-backed output.

### 2. [Verify deposit pricing and historical refunds (#29)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/29)

**Actions completed**

On 8 November the prototype introduced allocation origins and a history of formula
changes. The aim is for the ADA released on spending an output to equal the ADA
reserved at allocation, even after a rate change or a fork. Builders can use
the same pure calculation to work out the net deposit for a transaction. The prototype uses the simple V1 formula
agreed by Nicolas and Will. Experimental V2 demonstrates how a later formula
change applies to new allocations. The change passed 267 targeted examples.
On 9 November legacy deposit capping was made explicit, and the Store began
rejecting a return to V1 after V2 activation. A price change within either version
is still allowed. Historical V1 allocations continue to use V1.
[Formula history and pricing](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/660d0d96c24613a42cd3131a38a653ab1e39c6cf) · [Legacy cap](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/8029d18fb8f9bec696dbd1b7a1fabdba433468ec) · [Activation rule](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/2298a008589d5a0a5ea70bd1ec87d8477e989422)

**Next two weeks**

Trace the three steps in the implementation: select the formula at allocation
(the initial formula for outputs imported at migration), extract the required
fields from the output, and apply the formula. For example, spend a V1
allocation after V2 activation and check that its refund still uses V1. Check a legacy output whose calculated
deposit exceeds its ADA and verify that the cap leaves zero application ADA
while preserving the native assets.

### 3. [Review transition and ecosystem compatibility (#23)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/23)

**Actions completed**

Will's 4 November integration preserved total output value and exposed the
backing and application value separately in both V4 libraries. On a local
Conway-to-Dijkstra chain, historical replay, snapshot loading and recovery after
interruption passed. Three live V4 spends ran, alongside 46 ledger-context tests,
155 Plutus tests and 100 consensus property cases. Will recorded the revisions used for this fixture. [Integration and fixture](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-minutxo-integration/commit/d2300267436d4cc6a4cb2dbc7898b9f98787192b)

On 13 November Polina raised a concrete compatibility case on Slack. A validator
that checks an exact minimum-ADA amount could change its verdict if pricing
parameters change after signing. She proposed either exposing only application
value to scripts or binding the parameters into the script integrity hash so a
stale transaction is rejected before script execution. Both options remained
open at the end of the period. [Plutus discussion](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C07TXAT2H3R/p1794586660179989?thread_ts=1794586431.566559)

**Next two weeks**

1. **Assess the Plutus integration.** The review with Philip DiSarro, Will, Polina
   and Nicolas is planned for the week of 16 November. Nicolas asked Will to
   lead it. Use Will’s V4 fixture to check how a
   validator could read a legacy or a new input and distinguish its backing
   from its application value. Explore whether a future script version should
   see transaction-level deposit netting and how earlier validators would keep
   their existing semantics. These are questions for the integration review.
2. **Run a private-network test.** Create and spend a deposit-backed output

### 4. [Draft and calibrate the storage-cost and deposit proposals (#31)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/31)

**Actions completed**

On 9–10 November the team drafted the deposit proposal and split out the storage
cost formula. The formula can apply to existing minimum-ADA outputs. The
deposit proposal adds a second output form and lets a parent transaction fund
the deposits for its children. On 13 November Will opened the formula draft
for internal review. [Initial deposit draft](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/61) · [CIP split](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/63) · [Formula review](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/65)

Will tested the first formula, with a 250-byte base allowance, on 229,486,615
outputs created from Babbage through 28 October 2026. The median increase in
the required ADA per output was 13.7%. The formula gave a lower minimum for 6,756
outputs, all from Babbage. Outputs with many native assets showed the largest
increases. [13 November calibration](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-minutxo-integration/blob/9b5a105905611ee8d56c0fbc483eb7e40f14df98/docs/minutxo-calibration/history-formulas.md)

**Next two weeks**

Compare how the formula prices different address sizes and asset bundles.
Evaluate a builder-only estimate that avoids recalculating the minimum as ADA
is added. Present both CIPs at the ARC session on 16 November and circulate the
formula for review. [Alternative discussed on 12 November](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/D06FTPHMSNN/p1794490691029239)
