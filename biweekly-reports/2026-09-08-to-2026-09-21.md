# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 8–21 September 2026

**Status:** Consolidated draft — repository evidence, stream channel and discussions with Will reviewed.

## Executive summary

Building on the focused CPS and initial accounting work, this period moved the
CPS into official review and developed parallel split and transaction-netting
investigations. The Dijkstra split experiment
produced concrete findings on storage, migration and deposit calculation, but
integration remained incomplete. On 21 September, Nicolas parked this implementation
attempt and restarted from current Ledger master, carrying its findings forward
within the same exploration. Stream feedback also raised sizing
complexity and the incentives of account-backed outputs. Next steps are CPS review,
comparison of split/netting findings and clarification of these design constraints.

### 2. [Assess the output-split implementation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

The implementation attempt following the accounting refactor developed
separate output storage, migration using source protocol
parameters, and initial-funds allocation. It demonstrated that size-dependent
pricing can prevent an exact deposit/requirement match, motivating a smallest
sufficient deposit calculation. A
[checkpoint](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/commit/3c0614f26767ebe222d350b1b3f5f8688169175d)
was published in the personal fork. On 21 September, Nicolas parked this implementation
attempt and restarted from master; the findings remain useful, but the split is not delivered.

**Next two weeks**

Re-examine the output lifecycle and interfaces before choosing a revised approach.
Carry forward the constraints around value access, migration, pricing and script
visibility, and discuss their consequences with the netting track.

### 3. [Coordinate transaction-netting exploration](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41)

### 4. [Assess validation and design constraints](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/39)

**Actions completed**

Local checks recorded 402 passing examples across seven focused groups, while
compatibility and standard Dijkstra/API suites were blocked at compilation and CI
failed. On 18 September, Nicolas Biri relayed Pi's concerns about exact sizing and
fixed-point calculation. Discussion with Polina on 21 September exposed a further

controls spending and has no corresponding release incentive. This remains open.

**Next two weeks**

Validate integration alongside focused tests. Compare simpler sizing estimates
with developer needs, and examine funding, control and release incentives before
selecting protocol-account semantics.
