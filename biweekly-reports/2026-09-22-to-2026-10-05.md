# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 22 September–5 October 2026

**Status:** Interim draft as of 25 September — consolidated with Slack discussions and the recorded notes of the 24 September weekly.

**Tracking:** [Backlog](../backlog/README.md) · [M1 — CPS](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/3) · [M4 — Ledger prototype](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/4).

## Executive summary

Following the output-split experiments and the 21 September restart, the
24 September weekly aligned on exact deposit-release accounting. The working
direction is to move deposits out of UTxO structures to remove the fixed-point
dependency, retaining byte-based capacity pricing and dropping the proposed
formula change. The discussion identified changes to the transaction body and
support for legacy outputs. Will's netting assessment informs the design; Polina will examine nested
transactions and Ledger-rule implications. CPS-0037 remains under review, and
Nicolas plans to share a design draft with Dingo within two weeks of the meeting.

### 2. [Consolidate the output-split impact and accounting requirements](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

The weekly refined the impact map across accounting, staking, Plutus and builders.
Nicolas identified the transaction body as the proposed interface for deposit
injections and withdrawals, with an abstraction supporting legacy and new output
forms while preserving existing smart-contract lifecycles. Earlier checkpoints
include the
[direct split](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/3843077adc0ecf1d4ce92d92df00a0fe1586480b)
and the
[split following the accounting refactor](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/3c0614f26767ebe222d350b1b3f5f8688169175d),
both predating this reporting period.

The [24 September weekly notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CnqVT571qKzxJBVOdAAxCWM6QQc97C4SzBKseQVY8hQ/edit?tab=t.lfddgi8lle8)
record agreement to retain byte-based capacity pricing and drop the proposed
formula change. Moving deposits out of output structures is the proposed way to
remove the size/deposit dependency, while preserving exact allocation and release
accounting in the [protocol dust account](../backlog/issues/exploration/03-protocol-dust-account.md)
([#42](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/42)).
Token-count approximations miss large fields such as reference scripts; deposit
estimates must not leave residual amounts that cannot be withdrawn.

**Next two weeks**

Draft the allocation/release contract, transaction body interface and abstraction
for legacy and new outputs. Validate that the proposed structure removes the
fixed-point dependency, keeping funding, release rights, migration and script
visibility explicit. Review the Haskell and Agda codebases with Polina for
compatibility and alignment with Ledger rules.

### 3. [Review the transaction-netting assessment](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41)

**Actions completed**

Following the scope agreement of 16 September, Will shared his
[first assessment](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41#issuecomment-5796435821)
for review on 23 September, together with an
[annotated Ledger branch](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-ledger/commit/29586b32e2c4d663a2e4374410a808a07bcff521)
mapping affected code and required changes. Ordinary balancing can retain its
equation if totals include recorded backing; spending releases that recorded amount
rather than today's minimum. Changes concentrate on validation and transaction
building. At the weekly, Polina took responsibility for assessing interactions
between nested transactions, MinUTxO deposits and transaction builders, including
possible improvements. This extends Will's ordinary-transaction assessment.

**Next two weeks**

Align Will's assessment with the
[Value-split findings](../backlog/issues/exploration/01-output-role-split.md) and define
what explicit netting adds beyond existing balancing. Polina will assess the
nested-transaction interactions and report the implications for the shared design.
Feed the comparison into [M2 — Alternatives](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/2) and the shared
contract into [M3 — Solution design](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/1).
