# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 16–27 November 2026

**Status:** Interim draft through 26 November 2026.

## Executive summary

The team submitted the UTxO Storage Cost Formula to the Cardano CIP editors on
23 November. Applied to 85.7 million historical Conway outputs, the proposed
rule would raise the mean minimum from 1.43 to 1.89 ADA. The editors deferred
candidacy and asked Ledger and other node teams to assess the trade-off. The separate deposit proposal
reached a full ARC review draft. Its Ledger prototype passed CI for transactions
that move ADA between ordinary minimum-ADA outputs and separately funded
outputs. The next steps are to address the pricing feedback, settle what scripts
see and validate the updated prototype on a private network.

[Presentation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/66) · [All-hands notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qBMkhWFn7xOVqK9kBPcBCAIhXzhFCufJUANQcGUYc64/edit?tab=t.ih0jo839mhge) · [Slack review request](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C0BKY8ELKE2/p1795085112092699) · [Sample clarification](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C0BKY8ELKE2/p1795439479415739) · [Submission announcement](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/C0BKY8ELKE2/p1795446368899689)

The submitted formula uses a 193-byte base plus the actual address size, an
allowance per native asset, and the datum and reference-script sizes. A builder
can therefore calculate the minimum before choosing the ADA amount. Across
Conway mainnet outputs created through 28 October, every calculated minimum
met or exceeded the current rule. For outputs with six or more native assets,
the median increase was 80.7%. At their 24 November meeting the editors
deferred candidacy pending feedback on cost and implementation.
[Submitted CIP #1328](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/pull/1328) · [Calibration and alternatives](https://github.com/nhenin/CIPs/blob/ac37717c9820556db1b1bde2a25cfc6b85601e97/CIP-%3F%3F%3F%3F/README.md) · [Editors’ decision](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/pull/1328#pullrequestreview-6055567697)

### 2. [Specify the deposit rules and how builders use them (#31)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/31)

**Actions completed**

The deposit CIP now traces a transaction from construction through validation
and settlement. It explains how a builder recovers each input’s historical
reserve, prices new outputs and assigns funding or refunds between the parent
transaction (TopTx) and its children (SubTxs). A child that keeps a refund
identifies one of its own outputs in the signed transaction and must put enough
ADA there to cover the refund. The draft also covers collateral returns,
migration and price changes. In the prototype, an output with 8 ADA and a
separate 2 ADA deposit can contribute 8 ADA to stake and voting weight.
Nicolas shared the draft with Will on 25 November and the five-chapter Dijkstra rulebook on
26 November. The deposit CIP remains in ARC review.
[Deposit CIP and diagrams](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/64) · [Dijkstra rulebook](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/blob/77c14d0374100a0f3858bc567214bd62d30f795d/docs/dijkstra-utxo-deposit-rules/README.md) · [Rulebook handoff](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/D06FTPHMSNN/p1795687112881099)

**Next two weeks**

Agree what a Plutus validator sees for each output form. The Dijkstra prototype
exposes application value to V4. V1–V3 translations for deposit-backed outputs
remain open, including the case of a validator that checks an exact ADA amount.

### 3. [Verify reserve accounting across both output forms (#29)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/29)

**Actions completed**

On 24 November the builder and ledger began using the same calculation for
transactions that change output form. For example, spending an output with
1.6 ADA of embedded reserve to create one with a 2 ADA external deposit
requires a declaration of **0.4 ADA**. The external deposit store receives
the full **2 ADA**. Tests cover both directions, incorrect declarations and
delegation to the parent transaction.
[Mixed-output implementation](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/a7461ceb3fa828aa36e1bbf78c5021fe266d4614)

All 445 Ledger API examples and 219 focused DepositStore examples passed.
The complete Dijkstra run reported 1,452 examples, zero failures and two
pending, with one successful case per QuickCheck property. The full PR workflow
also passed at the 24 November implementation revision.
[Verification record](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/blob/77c14d0374100a0f3858bc567214bd62d30f795d/docs/dijkstra-utxo-deposit-rules/verification.md#signed-backing-change-for-mixed-output-forms--24-november-2026) · [Passing CI](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/actions/runs/45400929994)
