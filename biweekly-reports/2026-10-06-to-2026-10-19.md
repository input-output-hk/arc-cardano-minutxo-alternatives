# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 6–19 October 2026

**Tracking:** [Backlog](../backlog/README.md) · [M1 — CPS](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/3) · [M4 — Ledger prototype](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/4).

## Executive summary

The DepositStore prototype moved from output and transaction interfaces to recorded
ledger accounting, real spending scenarios and executable netting and balance rules.
Nicolas implemented per-output allocations, ordinary and collateral settlement, and
exact TopTx/SubTx declarations, while preserving SubTx fee contributions. By the
19 October committed checkpoint, 139 business checks passed and 117 remained
intentional placeholders; further local attribution work raised this to 144 passing
checks. Will progressed the downstream demonstration to Ledger submission and nested
transactions. CPS review continued, including attribution and earlier alternatives.
Next steps are to complete settlement and ledger-effect coverage, resolve the
refund-versus-fee interaction, and extend integration review.

### 2. [Consolidate the output-split impact and accounting requirements](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

The domain specification made allocation, recorded release, explicit delegation,
transaction-wide netting and collateral effects precise. It separates application
assets from UTxO capacity deposits and preserves the distinction between absent
and explicit-zero declarations. New deposits use
`coinsPerUTxOByte × (160 + accepted output size)`; consumption releases the recorded
allocation. The initial implementation still assumes fixed pricing parameters.

On [9 October](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/e10164c57ff90fb0d668cbef1eceda7b07b68b32),
declaration requirements were enforced for Store-backed output creation. The
[13 October accounting implementation](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/995ed4a4712a0c3db8b2cd7baae0a881970eb5a0)
then recorded allocations in ledger state and connected spending, exact declarations
and ordinary/collateral transfers to actual ledger execution. Tests fund and consume
real outputs, check one-lovelace errors, and distinguish ordinary success from
phase-2 failure. Snapshot support preserves the Store while retaining older-era
encoding. Compiling the conformance package does not establish an Agda model of
these new rules.

The [15 October Leios adaptation](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/e5da0be244308c217a9b0eecf221c797f4176f44)
rebased the prototype onto the updated Leios branch and restored compatibility with
its input accessors, state constructors and phase-2 conservation guard. The
specification and tests were reorganized around declarations, accounting and ledger
effects, with generated cases and independent expected results.

On 19 October, the
[declaration requirements](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/74849d422b6516a5fa3cfcd9d6177fbd2a5e4b38),
[exact accounting](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/b72b2bbc80570dc84c30b59b2498ceb80f6da5b7)
and [complete transaction balance](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/1d2aa2e3ff62cd7e0fd60c403811287f98335773)
checks were implemented. TopTx declares the complete net amount once; each SubTx
declares its own contribution. Tests preserve cross-child funding and SubTx fee
contributions. Ordinary financial conservation applies when phase 2 is valid;
the failure path follows collateral accounting.

Recorded validation progressed as follows; these are checkpoint-specific results:

An independent pristine-upstream comparison on 16 October reproduced the same

separate from the deliberate DepositStore placeholders. The overall regression
suite was not green; settlement and ledger-effect coverage also remained incomplete.

### 3. [Review transaction netting and downstream integration](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41)

**Actions completed**

The 6–7 October discussion with Polina clarified exact transaction-wide netting and
each child's contribution. It also exposed the unresolved strength of local release
settlement under CIP-0118 imbalances: requiring an output to contain the released
amount does not establish refund provenance or preserve every fee-funding pattern.

Will's downstream review identified output discriminators in API/RPC, database
migrations and wallet/balancer validation changes. In his
[9 October update](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/D06FTPHMSNN/p1791551617427229),
a fresh multi-repository workspace submitted implicit-deposit and Store-backed
transactions to Ledger validation; the examples still ended in intentional errors.
On 14 October he shared a
[nested-transaction example](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-minutxo-integration/blob/minutxo-incremental/checks/transaction/Nested.hs).
These are concrete integration checkpoints, not a completed downstream rollout.

The [19 October refund example](https://input-output-rnd.slack.com/archives/D06FTPHMSNN/p1792423176903689)
made the open fee interaction concrete: 10 ADA of old backing funded 8 ADA of new
backing, a 0.2 ADA fee and a 1.8 ADA spendable refund, while strict settlement
required the full 2 ADA release in the designated output. The issue was still open at the reporting
cutoff; exact netting and global balance alone do not resolve it.

**Next two weeks**

Resolve the refund/fee semantics with Will and Polina, preserving existing SubTx
imbalance and fee contributions. Continue the nested-transaction and before/after
demonstrations, including transaction reading, and identify the remaining API,
database and wallet changes. Feed the findings into
[M2 — Alternatives](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/2)
and [M3 — Solution design](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/1),
with Agda terminology and the stronger settlement question explicitly tracked.
