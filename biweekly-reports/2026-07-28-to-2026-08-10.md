# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 28 July–10 August 2026

## Executive summary

The first two weeks focused on exploring the min-UTxO problem, understanding the
current mechanism, and collecting the solution proposals discussed by the team. The
work clarified the scope of the investigation and organized the main questions that
need to be addressed. No final architecture or policy recommendation was selected.
The next step was to consolidate the collected material, strengthen the problem
statement, and establish a common basis for comparing the proposed directions.

## Goals

### 1. [Establish the stream scope and working structure](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/4)

**Actions completed**

The initial scope of the min-UTxO stream was established and
the working repository was organized. The problem-definition and solution-exploration
tracks were identified, together with the main open questions and follow-up work.

**Next two weeks**

Consolidate the problem-definition work, strengthen
the technical evidence, and turn the initial questions into a structured work plan.

### 2. [Review the current mechanism and initial source material](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/5)

**Actions completed**

The current min-UTxO mechanism, its historical motivation, and
the initial technical source material were reviewed. The investigation distinguished
the persistent-state concern from the way the current requirement is exposed to
applications and transaction builders, and recorded the claims requiring verification.

**Next two weeks**

Develop a clearer problem statement, verify the
principal historical and technical claims, and identify the empirical evidence needed
for the CPS.

### 3. [Collect solution proposals and define the exploration plan](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/6)

**Actions completed**

The solution proposals raised during team discussions were
collected and organized. Their main questions were recorded without selecting an
implementation, and the need for a common evaluation framework and complete candidate
designs was identified.

**Next two weeks**

Describe the proposed implementations consistently,
define the common evaluation criteria, and begin constructing complete candidates for
comparison.
