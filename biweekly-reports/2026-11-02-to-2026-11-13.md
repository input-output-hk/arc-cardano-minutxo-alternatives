### 2. [Verify deposit pricing and historical refunds (#29)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/29)

**Actions completed**

On 8 November the prototype introduced allocation origins and a history of formula
changes. A refund uses the formula that applied to that output. The implementation
includes V1 and an experimental V2. The change passed 267 targeted examples.
On 9 November legacy deposit capping was made explicit, and the Store began
rejecting a return to V1 after V2 activation. A price change within either version
is still allowed. Historical V1 allocations continue to use V1.
[Formula history and pricing](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/660d0d96c24613a42cd3131a38a653ab1e39c6cf) · [Legacy cap](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/8029d18fb8f9bec696dbd1b7a1fabdba433468ec) · [Activation rule](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/2298a008589d5a0a5ea70bd1ec87d8477e989422)

**Next two weeks**

Trace the three steps in the implementation: select the formula at allocation
(the initial formula for outputs imported at migration), extract the required
fields from the output, and apply the formula. For example, spend a V1
allocation after V2 activation and check that its refund still uses V1. Check a legacy output whose calculated
deposit exceeds its ADA and verify that the cap leaves zero application ADA
while preserving the native assets.

### 3. [Review transition and ecosystem compatibility (#23)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/23)

**Actions completed**

Will's 4 November integration preserved total output value and exposed the
backing and application value separately in both V4 libraries. On a local
Conway-to-Dijkstra chain, historical replay, snapshot loading and recovery after
interruption passed. Three live V4 spends ran, alongside 46 ledger-context tests,
155 Plutus tests and 100 consensus property cases. Will recorded the revisions used for this fixture. [Integration and fixture](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-minutxo-integration/commit/d2300267436d4cc6a4cb2dbc7898b9f98787192b)

**Next two weeks**

1. **Assess the Plutus integration.** Use Will’s V4 fixture to check how a
   validator could read a legacy or a new input and distinguish its backing
   from its application value. Explore whether a future script version should
   see transaction-level deposit netting and how earlier validators would keep
   their existing semantics. These are questions for the integration review.
2. **Run a private-network test.** Create and spend a deposit-backed output
   using the reviewed revisions. Record the transaction IDs and compare the
   deposit charged and the ADA refunded with the expected amounts.
