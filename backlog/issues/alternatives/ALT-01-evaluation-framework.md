# 01 — Define the evaluation framework and invariants

**Milestone:** [M2 — Implementation Design Space Analysis & Policy Recommendation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/2) · **Status:** In progress

## Outcome

A common scorecard for evaluating every candidate architecture.

## Acceptance criteria

- Required invariants cover bounded state, determinism, conservation, solvency,
  unique release, and legacy-claim safety.
- Criteria cover abstraction, liquidity, staking, compatibility, governance, and cost.
- Capacity assumptions and empirical inputs link back to the CPS.
- Mandatory requirements are separated from preferences and implementation choices.
