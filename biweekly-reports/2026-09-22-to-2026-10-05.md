## Executive summary

Following the output-split experiments and the 21 September restart, work moved
to consolidating their findings with Will's first netting assessment. The
24 September weekly established a broad map of affected interfaces, workflow,
modules and concepts. It identified resolution of the fixed-point problem as
necessary for precise deposit-release accounting in the proposed protocol dust
account. Polina will analyse possible improvements around nested transactions.
In parallel, the submitted CPS received number CPS-0037 and remains under review.
The next step is to turn these findings into shared design contracts while keeping
alternative solutions open.

### 2. [Consolidate the output-split impact and accounting requirements](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

The weekly consolidated the affected construction/read/update interfaces and the
workflow through validation, storage, spending and migration, including accounting,
staking, Plutus and builder boundaries. Earlier Git checkpoints include the
[direct split](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/3843077adc0ecf1d4ce92d92df00a0fe1586480b)
and the
[split following the accounting refactor](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/3c0614f26767ebe222d350b1b3f5f8688169175d),
both predating this reporting period.

The meeting established that the fixed-point problem must be resolved to support
precise deposit release if backing moves to a
[protocol dust account](../backlog/issues/exploration/03-protocol-dust-account.md)
([#42](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/42)). Excluding the
deposit from charged size remains a candidate solution. Optional conservative
estimates for builders were also discussed; no final sizing rule was selected.

**Next two weeks**

Specify precise allocation and release accounting for the dust-account design,
then evaluate how to remove the fixed-point dependency. Keep funding, control of
spending and release incentives explicit in that contract. Refine the mapped interfaces
into implementation steps, preserving migration and script-visibility constraints.

### 3. [Review the transaction-netting assessment](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41)

**Actions completed**

Following the scope agreement of 16 September, Will shared his
[first assessment](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41#issuecomment-5796435821)
for review on 23 September, together with an
[annotated Ledger branch](https://github.com/willjgould/cardano-ledger/commit/29586b32e2c4d663a2e4374410a808a07bcff521)
mapping affected code and required changes. Ordinary balancing can retain its
equation if totals include recorded backing; spending releases that recorded amount
rather than today's minimum. Changes concentrate on validation and transaction
building. At the weekly, Polina took responsibility for analysing possible
improvements around nested transactions. This extends the exploration beyond
Will's ordinary-transaction assessment.

**Next two weeks**

Align Will's assessment with the
[Value-split findings](../backlog/issues/exploration/01-output-role-split.md) and define
what explicit netting adds beyond existing balancing. Polina will assess the
nested-transaction opportunities and their implications for the shared design.
Feed the comparison into [M2 — Alternatives](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/2) and the shared
contract into [M3 — Solution design](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/1).
