# Milestone M2 — Implementation Design Space Analysis & Policy Recommendation

**State:** In progress

## Objective

Compare complete architectures against a shared framework and produce the final,
evidence-backed policy recommendation.

## Scope

- Security, solvency, determinism, and migration invariants.
- Four-layer candidates: capacity control, settlement, representation, transition.
- Liquidity, staking, compatibility, governance, and implementation trade-offs.
- Quantitative sensitivity to CPS evidence and capacity assumptions.
- Final recommendation and rationale for rejected alternatives.

## Issues

- [01 — Define the evaluation framework and invariants](../issues/alternatives/ALT-01-evaluation-framework.md)
- [02 — Construct complete candidate architectures](../issues/alternatives/ALT-02-candidate-architectures.md)
- [03 — Analyze trade-offs and compare implementations](../issues/alternatives/ALT-03-tradeoff-analysis.md)
- [04 — Write the final policy recommendation](../issues/alternatives/ALT-04-policy-report.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- Candidates are complete architectures, evaluated with the same evidence and criteria.
- Credits have solvent backing and legacy state gains no unfunded claim.
- Sensitivity and uncertainties are explicit.
- One final policy recommendation is approved for CIP specification.

## Out of scope

Normative CIP wording and `cardano-ledger` implementation.
