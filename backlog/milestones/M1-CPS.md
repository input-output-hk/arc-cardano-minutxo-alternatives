# Milestone M1 — Problem Statement Definition (CPS)

**Deliverable:** Cardano Problem Statement

**Working artifact:** `CPS-????/README.md`

**State:** In progress

## Objective

Define the min-UTxO problem accurately and solution-neutrally, with enough public and
reproducible evidence to establish why the application-facing abstraction should be improved
without weakening the adversarial bound on persistent ledger state.

## Scope

- Current mechanism and historical provenance.
- Mainnet state, churn, minimum-ada incidence, and staking evidence.
- Solution-neutral capacity and allocation/release model.
- Representation, responsibility, and lifecycle mismatches.
- Concrete use cases, required outcomes, non-goals, and open questions.

## Epic and sub-issues

The milestone is organized through the [Problem Statement Definition (CPS) epic](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/7),
with six outcome-oriented sub-issues covering the current mechanism, formal model, empirical
evidence, use cases, requirements, review, and submission. See the
[backlog index](../README.md#m1--cps) for the complete list.

## Milestone acceptance criteria

- Problem and evidence are separable from any preferred solution.
- Quantitative claims are reproducible or explicitly qualified.
- Concrete use cases are publicly citable.
- Notation, diagrams, references, and historical claims pass domain review.
- Authors approve a CPS-editor-ready submission.

## Out of scope

Selecting the final mechanism, normative CIP rules, and ledger implementation.
