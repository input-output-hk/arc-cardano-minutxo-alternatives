# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 25 August–7 September 2026

**Status:** In progress — evidence recorded through 1 September 2026

## Executive summary

Across the first month, the CPS draft evolved as context review, Buildfest follow-up,
mainnet analysis, and codebase investigation improved the stream's understanding of
the problem. During this period, that work converged on a first focused draft covering
the application and transaction-builder friction of the current implementation. In
parallel, an active Dijkstra codebase exploration implemented the output-role split
directly to expose its consequences. That spike revealed a broader prerequisite: the
current `Value` abstraction carries several distinct asset, coin, and mint concepts
without enough domain granularity. Work therefore restarted from `master` to introduce
narrower concepts before repeating the split on a clearer foundation. Together, these
tracks established a concrete three-step direction to propose for a future CIP:
separate application assets from the deposit in outputs, make transaction-level
deposit netting explicit, and investigate moving deposit accounting into a
protocol-managed dust account. This is a proposed roadmap, not an adopted
architecture. A follow-up meeting with Adam Dean on 31 August confirmed that this
reconstruction was consistent with the direction discussed at Buildfest. The immediate
priorities are public evidence and domain review for the CPS, completion of the
preparatory domain refactor, formalization of the staged architecture, and
deposit-aware end-to-end tests. On 1 September,
[PR #37](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/pull/37) also merged the backlog
correction that points to the canonical root epic, issue #1.

## Goals

**Actions completed**

The CPS was re-scoped around one causal claim: the current implementation blends
operational backing for persistent state with the applicative value governed by an
output's spending condition. The active `CPS-????/README.md` now distinguishes
necessary resource friction from accidental implementation friction and organizes
the problem around cases including the frozen wallet, sub-minimum payments,
sender-funded ADA, narrow non-staking application state, high-fan-out distribution,
transaction construction, and continuing-output top-ups. Existing mitigations and
residual trade-offs are explicit. The previous broad CPS was archived at

### 2. [Produce and validate the mainnet evidence](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/10)

**Actions completed**

The exact history was narrowed to the evidence needed by the scoped CPS, using the
frozen DB-Sync series through completed epoch 648 on 12 August 2026. Across epochs
503–648, mainnet created 78,109,428 outputs and consumed 78,151,416 while the live set
fell by 41,988 outputs, or 0.38% of the opening count. Gross turnover was 156,260,844
events and epoch 648 ended with 11,076,329 live outputs, 1.50% below the epoch-511
peak. A two-panel `09-live-utxo-history.svg` now shows live stock and recent flows.
The text explicitly treats this as descriptive coexistence of high turnover and count
stability, not proof that minUTxO caused the plateau or that the observed state is
safe.

**Next two weeks**

review the date and epoch mapping, and keep causal interpretation, tariff calibration,
and operational safety thresholds outside the descriptive claim.

### 3. [Construct complete candidate architectures](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/16)

**Actions completed**

The CPS and prototype work produced a concrete three-stage candidate roadmap for the
stream:

1. **Separate output roles.** Represent application-controlled `Assets` and the
   `CapacityDeposit` in separate fields of the same output. `Assets` includes
   application-controlled ADA as well as native assets. This makes the two concerns
   explicit while retaining a deposit associated with each UTxO.
2. **Introduce transaction-level netting.** Make deposit allocation and release
   explicit at the transaction boundary. Transaction builders would balance the sum
   of the recorded backing released by consumed outputs against the deposits required
   by created outputs, changing how funding, change, and continuing outputs are
   constructed.
3. **Move settlement to a protocol dust account.** Move the backing ADA out of
   individual application output values and investigate a protocol-managed account
   responsible for the aggregate deposit-allocation and release logic. `Dust account`
   is the current working name for this protocol-level component. The design may still
   need a per-UTxO receipt or policy reference for deterministic release and repricing.

This sequencing is intended to make each architectural step independently reviewable.
The first stage is represented by the current Dijkstra experiment; transaction netting
and the dust account remain design directions rather than implemented or adopted
semantics. The release beneficiary, repricing, staking, solvency, and migration remain
open.

The architectural roadmap and the engineering order are distinct. The direct Dijkstra
split exposed a codebase prerequisite that now comes first: untangle the overloaded
`Value` abstraction into narrower asset-, coin-, and mint-related concepts, then
reimplement the output-role split on that clearer domain model.

A follow-up meeting with Adam Dean on 31 August confirmed that this reconstruction of
the staged direction was consistent with what had been discussed at Buildfest. This
closes part of the provenance gap left by the absence of a shared written Buildfest
record; it does not constitute formal adoption of the architecture by the stream.

**Next two weeks**

Specify the invariants, interfaces, and transition assumptions of each stage. Separate
what the current prototype demonstrates from what remains hypothetical, compare the
staged direction against the other recovered Buildfest proposals, and seek stream
review before treating it as the basis of a future CIP.

### 4. [Align the domain model with the adopted specification](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/26)

**Actions completed**

The prototype work proceeded through two consecutive explorations. First, the Dijkstra
experiment implemented the output split directly to understand which types,
interfaces, accounting paths, translations, and tests it would affect. Once that split
was understood, it exposed a deeper problem: the codebase uses `Value` for several
different concepts, including output assets, ADA projections, signed mint deltas, and
policy projections. A global `Value -> NewName` substitution would preserve that
conceptual ambiguity rather than solve it.

The implementation order was therefore revised. Work restarted from `master` on the
separate
[`nicolas/minutxo-accounting-refactor`](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/tree/nicolas/minutxo-accounting-refactor)
branch to introduce narrower domain concepts before returning to the output split. The
exploratory vocabulary now includes `Assets era`, `CapacityDeposit`, `OutputCoins`,
`MintDelta`, `MintedAssets`, and `BurnedAssets`. `Assets` includes
application-controlled ADA as well as native assets, while `CapacityDeposit` denotes
the distinct operational role. Five signed commits on 31 August, culminating in
[`6084525881`](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/commit/6084525881bbb60b1ec4b237cbc4b5fd5616809c),
introduced typed mint views and propagated them through UTxO accounting, policy
projection, Plutus translation, and the Babbage upgrade boundary. This is preparatory
interface research; issue #26 remains blocked by the unselected M3 design.

**Next two weeks**

Complete the production `Value era` inventory, finish the in-progress `TxInfo`
propagation, and determine whether any real domain operation needs a whole-type
combination of application assets and capacity deposits rather than explicit ADA
projections or separate conservation equations.

### 5. [Integrate the mechanism into Ledger rules](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/27)

**Actions completed**

On the exploration branch
[`nicolas/minutxo-deposit`](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/tree/nicolas/minutxo-deposit),
commit
[`3843077`](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/commit/3843077adc0ecf1d4ce92d92df00a0fe1586480b)
integrated the output split into the Dijkstra experiment. New split outputs carry
explicit capacity deposits, while Babbage-map and Alonzo-array legacy forms remain
decodable. Legacy outputs are restructured when translated or admitted to Dijkstra
UTxO state, preserving total ADA. Consumed and produced accounting was split into
asset and capacity-deposit components, and the phase-2-invalid collateral path was
corrected to operate on total ADA. This remains an exploratory implementation of
candidate semantics, not an adopted solution. The policy audit also confirmed that
a live UTxO records neither its creation era nor the historical
`coinsPerUTxOByte` that applied when it was created. Exact release from a global
off-output reserve therefore requires a per-UTxO receipt or immutable policy
reference, or an explicitly floating repricing rule backed by a separately proven
solvency mechanism. Issue #27 remains blocked by M3; this experiment does not satisfy
its integration outcome.

**Next two weeks**

Make builders and Imp tests deposit-aware, resolve deposit-blind ADA-pot accounting,
define a safe translation for the inherited output-too-small failure, and keep all
compatibility adapters confined to explicit era and generic-interface boundaries.
