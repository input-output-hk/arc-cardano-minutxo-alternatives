# 02 — Construct complete candidate architectures

**Milestone:** [M2 — Implementation Design Space Analysis & Policy Recommendation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/milestone/2) · **Status:** In progress

## Outcome

A shortlist of internally coherent candidates, each defined across capacity control,
economic settlement, ledger representation, and transition.

## Acceptance criteria

- Current embedded minimum-ada is the baseline.
- Ledger reserve, credential-backed, hard-cap/state-credit, and hybrid families are covered.
- Each candidate defines pricing, beneficiary, backing, repricing, and migration.
- Fees and negative fees are not mistaken for complete capacity-control mechanisms.
- Dust accounts remain adjacent architecture unless a complete tuple is demonstrated.
