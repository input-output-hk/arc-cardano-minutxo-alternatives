# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 28 July–10 August 2026

## Executive summary

The opening period focused on building a high-level understanding of the assignment,
situating the min-UTxO and related CIP work within the broader Ledger and application
context, and clarifying what was in and out of scope. Because the proposals discussed
at Buildfest had no shared written record, work also began to reconstruct their
intended mechanisms and assumptions through conversations with participants. This was
a discovery phase: no architecture or policy direction was selected. The next step
was to turn that context into a sharper problem statement, an evidence plan, and a
consistent basis for comparing the recovered proposals.

## Goals

### 1. [Establish the stream scope and working structure](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/4)

**Actions completed**

The initial scope of the min-UTxO stream was established and the working repository
was organized. The assignment was situated within the wider min-UTxO and CIP context,
and the boundary between problem definition, solution exploration, and work outside
the stream was made explicit. The main open questions and follow-up work were then
organized around that initial framing.

**Next two weeks**

Validate the initial scope, examine the current codebase to understand the likely
implementation consequences, strengthen the technical evidence, and turn the initial
questions into a structured work plan.

### 2. [Review the current mechanism and initial source material](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/5)

**Actions completed**

The current min-UTxO mechanism, its historical motivation, and
the initial technical source material were reviewed. The investigation distinguished
the persistent-state concern from the way the current requirement is exposed to
applications and transaction builders, and recorded the claims requiring verification.

**Next two weeks**

Develop a clearer problem statement, verify the
principal historical and technical claims, and identify the empirical evidence needed
for the CPS.

### 3. [Collect solution proposals and define the exploration plan](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-minutxo/issues/6)

**Actions completed**

The solution proposals discussed at Buildfest existed only in participants'
recollection rather than in a shared design record. Follow-up conversations began
reconstructing their intended mechanisms, assumptions, unresolved questions, and the
different views held by participants. The material was recorded without treating any
proposal as selected, exposing the need for a common description and evaluation
framework before meaningful comparison could begin.

**Next two weeks**

Continue the participant interviews, record provenance and unresolved disagreements,
describe the proposed implementations consistently, and begin testing their
implications against the current codebase.
