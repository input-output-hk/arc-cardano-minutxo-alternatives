## Executive summary

Attempt #3 progressed from the core-interface experiment to output adaptations
through Shelley, Allegra, Mary, Alonzo, Babbage, Conway and Dijkstra. The published
prototype represents implicit-deposit and store-backed outputs and exposes
transaction-level deposit declarations, explicit SubTx delegation and settlement
targets. Focused codec and compatibility checks passed, including the repair of
six Dijkstra CDDL failures. CPS-0037 review progressed with a published reply on
prior contributions and fork revisions covering parameter-change accounting.
The next step is to implement DepositStore state and accounting behind these
interfaces, while continuing downstream and protocol review. Those rules were
not enforced at the 5 October cutoff.

### 2. [Consolidate the output-split impact and accounting requirements](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

**Attempt #3: explicit output variants and interface separation.** Building on the
earlier direct-split and storage-separation experiments, Nicolas moved the
distinction to `TxOut era`: legacy implicit-deposit outputs and new store-backed
outputs. This lets a transaction consume legacy outputs and create new ones while
retaining the legacy representation. Shared operations belong to `EraTxOut`;
variant-specific operations have separate interfaces, and application assets have
a distinct type.

The initial core interface was committed on 25 September on `v2-minutxo`, based
on the Leios prototype. Shared coin operations and UTxO aggregation handle both
variants, with the core library compiling at
[37b85d385](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/37b85d385d3b00e0664ef02e33f8c8738f7f5bd8).
The [draft PR](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/pull/2) and
[checkpoint in #40](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40#issuecomment-5866268906)
were shared on 28 September.

On 28–29 September, the interfaces were propagated through Shelley, Allegra, Mary,
Alonzo, Babbage and Conway, retaining their implicit-deposit outputs and historical
encodings. On 2 October,
[Dijkstra storage and consumers](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/39019ba5624d2429c210003f7e16d827fee19c88)
were adapted across accounting, Plutus V4 translation, the public API, state
queries, generators, tests and benchmarks. The
[CDDL update](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/00ede97526f48f91908a24c557f5e50c0da58300)
added the store-backed output format and its `application_assets` grammar.

The [5 October transaction interfaces](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/ee56dc0467ba7dcd467a2b73166f1c7a270f9bba)
added TopTx and SubTx declarations, including explicit requests for TopTx funding
and delegated release settlement. A
[further refinement](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/b5583790b8d22d883042a5bc3348d5a54de7f37d)
distinguished absence from explicit zero, required positive amounts for nonzero
operations, and represented local, delegated and mixed settlement. Public API,
CBOR/JSON codecs and CDDL were updated. Nicolas announced the published interface
checkpoint on 5 October, explicitly leaving DepositStore state, accounting and
validation for subsequent work. #40 and M4 remain open.

The 24 September design discussion favoured excluding the deposit from the priced
output to remove the size/deposit dependency. Exact byte pricing and conservative
tooling estimates remained under discussion; no replacement pricing rule was
agreed in that exchange. Datum and reference-script sizes must be considered
alongside token counts.

Recorded local checks progressed beyond the earlier compilation blockers:

These are interface and compatibility results. DepositStore accounting was not
