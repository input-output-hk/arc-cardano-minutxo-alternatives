# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 25 August–7 September 2026

**Status:** Consolidated draft — repository evidence and Slack discussions reviewed.

## Executive summary

Building on the initial mechanism analysis and reconstruction of proposals, the
CPS was narrowed to application and transaction-builder friction and published
in the ARC repository. Review identified an error in the historical UTxO stock
calculation, so the chart and related claims were removed. Ledger exploration
established narrower accounting interfaces and a first explicit output-allocation
model. An initial exploration direction was documented across three levels: output-role
separation, transaction deposit netting and protocol-managed settlement. Next steps
were external CPS review and investigation of the interfaces connecting these levels.

## Goals

### 1. [Prepare the CPS for external review](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/12)

**Actions completed**

The focused CPS was published through
[PR #38](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/38)
on 2 September and shared with the stream for review of use cases and workarounds.
Revisions on 7 September clarified seven use cases and their
workarounds, qualified the causal claims, and explained funding constraints after
capacity-price changes. The historical stock chart was withdrawn: the reconstruction
omitted genesis outputs while counting their later consumption. No corrected stock
count was established.

**Next two weeks**

Complete the terminology and open-question review, prepare the official submission,
and seek Ledger, wallet and application feedback. Keep stock correction and named
application evidence as explicit follow-up work.

### 2. [Structure the three-level exploration](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/39)

**Actions completed**

A follow-up with Adam Dean confirmed the reconstruction of the Buildfest direction.
[PR #44](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/pull/44),
merged on 3 September, defined the specification, design and evidence expected for
output separation, deposit netting and protocol settlement. These are cumulative
exploration levels forming the workstream's initial direction; detailed
contracts and comparative justification remain open.

**Next two weeks**

Map the affected Ledger interfaces and establish shared assumptions between the
three levels, including funding, release rights, conservation and migration.

### 3. [Explore output and accounting interfaces](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

The early
[direct split experiment](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/commit/3843077adc0ecf1d4ce92d92df00a0fe1586480b),
dated 27 August, exposed ambiguity between application holdings, total ada and
mint/burn quantities. A subsequent approach began with an accounting refactor. The
[3 September forging checkpoint](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger/commit/a0d67967bffb0667bf9665db0e74422dc70bba10)
introduced narrower mint interfaces across accounting, translation and API consumers.
An explicit output-allocation model followed on 7 September, separating accounting
totals from mutable value access. This remained exploratory work, without a validated
end-to-end split implementation. On 7 September, Nicolas shared the CPS and forging
checkpoint with Will to begin joint codebase exploration.

**Next two weeks**

Investigate Dijkstra storage, Plutus visibility, collateral and migration. Specify
construction, read and update behavior separately, and validate affected integration
paths alongside focused tests.
