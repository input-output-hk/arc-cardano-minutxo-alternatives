# Milestone M5 — CIP Authoring & Submission

## Objective

Produce the final CIP from the reviewed solution specification and integrated prototype findings.

## Sub-issues

1. Draft the CIP specification sections.
2. Write the rationale and alternatives-considered sections.
3. Document compatibility, transition, and implementation path.
4. Complete domain and editorial review.
5. Prepare the CIP submission.

## Acceptance criteria

- The CIP matches the reviewed M3 specification and incorporates M4 findings.
- Normative specification, rationale, compatibility, transition, and implementation are complete.
- Authors and domain reviewers approve the document.
- Metadata, license, references, number, and submission package are correct.
