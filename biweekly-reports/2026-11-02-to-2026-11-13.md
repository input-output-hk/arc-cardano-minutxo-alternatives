# ARC min-UTxO — Bi-weekly report

**Reporting period:** 2–13 November 2026

**Status:** Interim draft through 9 November 2026.

## Executive summary

The Ledger prototype integrated the 30 October Leios snapshot. The 27 SubTx
cases that failed in the previous period passed locally, and the full CI workflow
passed again on 8 November. Outputs can now retain their creation-time pricing
through a formula change. In a separate integration, Will demonstrated three live
Plutus V4 spends and recovered a migrated local chain from a snapshot. The next
decisions concern how precisely the deposit should track output size, which values
older scripts can observe, and which scenarios still need a private-network run.

### 2. [Compare deposit-pricing alternatives (#17)](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/17)

**Actions completed**

On 8 November the prototype introduced allocation origins and a history of formula
changes. A refund uses the formula that applied to that output. The implementation
includes V1 and an experimental V2. The change passed 267 targeted examples.
On 9 November legacy deposit capping was made explicit, and the Store began
rejecting a return to V1 after V2 activation. A price change within either version
is still allowed. Historical V1 allocations continue to use V1.
[Formula history and pricing](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/660d0d96c24613a42cd3131a38a653ab1e39c6cf) · [Legacy cap](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/8029d18fb8f9bec696dbd1b7a1fabdba433468ec) · [Activation rule](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/2298a008589d5a0a5ea70bd1ec87d8477e989422)

**Next two weeks**

Compare two properties on the same UTxO sample. Measure how much extra ADA each candidate
requires and what a builder must serialize or recompute to obtain the deposit.
Record the baseline, sample, median and upper percentiles so reviewers can
reproduce the comparison before selecting a formula.
