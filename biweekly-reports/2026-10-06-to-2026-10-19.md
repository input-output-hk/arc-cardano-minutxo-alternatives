Work moved from the published output and transaction interfaces to a precise
DepositStore domain model and executable specification. Nicolas defined exact
allocation/release accounting, transaction-wide netting, explicit SubTx delegation,
collateral settlement and Store solvency, while preserving existing SubTx fee
contributions. The test outline is organized into 21 domain-rule modules. The
first declaration rule is implemented locally with nine passing focused checks;
163 checks remain intentional placeholders. Will's downstream review identified
representation and validation changes in API/RPC, database and wallet packages.
Next steps are ledger-state integration, exact-accounting enforcement and
resolution of collateral and local-settlement gaps. #40, #41 and M4 remain open.

### 2. [Consolidate the output-split impact and accounting requirements](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/40)

**Actions completed**

The [6 October checkpoint](https://github.com/nhenin/cardano-ledger-specs/commit/2c1f6b369c395f431b273149120992cddc93c4bd)
expanded the DepositStore rules into an explicit accounting and lifecycle model.
Nicolas refined this with Polina's questions and the executable-spec work. The
current prototype contract specifies:

- **Exact allocation and release.** Record each store-backed output's allocated
  UTxO capacity deposit in its live UTxO entry, separately from its application
  assets. New deposits use `coinsPerUTxOByte × (160 + accepted output size)`;
  spending releases the recorded amount. Deposit metadata stays outside the
  priced output. Declarations must be exact, with no voluntary excess.
- **Netting and delegation.** Each body's net contribution is newly allocated
  deposits minus released deposits. TopTx declares the total once, as one net
  allocation, release or explicit zero. SubTx declarations must match their own
  contributions; explicit delegation changes where those contributions are
  accounted for. SubTxs may remain financially imbalanced and contribute to TopTx
  fees, provided the complete transaction balances.
- **Collateral and state transitions.** Ordinary success, accepted phase-2 failure
  and rejection have separate effects. Collateral funds any additional deposit
  required by its return before covering the minimum collateral fee. Excess
  released deposits go to fees. Rejection preserves ledger state.
- **Store solvency and lifecycle.** The Store balance must cover the sum of deposits
  allocated to live store-backed outputs. Consumption and creation update the
  deposit records and Store balance together. The initial increment assumes fixed
  pricing parameters; historical pricing must eventually be recoverable.

On 8 October, local commit `7f0b5e5af` organized the executable outline into
**21 domain-rule modules**. Each module keeps its scenarios and mathematical
properties together, using shared terminology for UTxO capacity deposits,
allocation, release and settlement. The committed outline contained 170 deliberate
placeholders, making the implementation backlog explicit.

**Implementation in progress:** The working tree separates TopTx and SubTx
declarations into distinct types with qualified, semantic names for absence,
explicit zero, allocation, release and delegation. Fixtures generate cases;
adapters prepare and submit transactions; the spec modules express the domain
rules. The first rule, **DS-TX-009**, is connected to ledger validation: any explicit
SubTx declaration, including zero, requires a TopTx declaration. These implementation
and test refinements remain local and uncommitted.

Recorded focused validation passed:

| Check | Result and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Six ledger scenarios | Absent/zero declaration combinations, including phase-2-invalid acceptance and rejection. |
| Two isolated validator examples | Cancelling contributions and local/delegated operations still require a TopTx declaration. |
| One generated property | 100 samples, each checking 115 declaration combinations against an independent expected result. |
| Declaration codecs | 52 TopTx and 38 SubTx examples passed during the declaration-type refactor. |
| Serialization regression | JSON, transaction-body and predicate-failure round trips passed, together with the predicate-failure golden. |

The outline now contains **172 checks: nine implemented checks with recorded

### 3. [Review transaction netting and downstream integration](https://github.com/input-output-hk/arc-cardano-minutxo-alternatives/issues/41)

**Actions completed**

The 6–7 October discussion with Polina clarified that TopTx declares the exact
net change for the complete transaction, while each SubTx declares its own
contribution. Deposit 5 and release 3 intentionally becomes one net allocation of
2. The discussion also exposed an open issue: a settlement output containing at
least the released amount does not, by itself, define a stronger local-settlement
guarantee under CIP-0118 imbalances. Existing SubTx fee contributions must remain
possible; no separate financial-balance equality is being imposed on every SubTx.

Will's 7–8 October downstream investigation identified:

- `cardano-api` / `cardano-rpc`: Protobuf `TxOutput` needs a discriminator to preserve
  implicit-deposit versus store-backed outputs. Ledger CBOR/JSON and MemPack
  distinctions do not automatically survive this mapping.
- `db-sync`: the `tx_out` and `collateral_tx_out` tables need a migration to record
  the distinction.
- `cardano-wallet` / `cardano-balance-transaction`: output types and
  `validateTxOutputs'` in asset selection need to account for the new variant.

These are identified changes, not completed downstream integrations. The design
also exposes a use case: a SubTx can transfer native tokens while the TopTx funds
fees and UTxO capacity deposits. This remains a capability to validate through the
integrated accounting rules.
